About the Founder

A Personal Mission,
Not Just a Career

D. Romano's path to personal injury law began with two profound losses in childhood. His paternal grandfather — an immigrant who came to America alone as a teenager — worked under dangerous conditions in the coal mines of West Virginia and later died of complications from those injuries. Around the same time, his maternal grandfather died of heart and lung disease from a lifetime of smoking, in an era when tobacco companies advertised that smoking was healthy. Neither family saw any legal remedy or compensation.

"I like to believe that the Romano Law Firm is the firm that my grandmothers and family would have consulted — if they'd only been given the chance."

— D. Romano, Founder

Romano earned a business degree in economics, then a J.D. from Florida State University College of Law in 1992, where he was selected for the Law Review — a distinction reserved for high-achieving students. He began his career at firms defending insurance companies and large business interests, which gave him a clear understanding of how insurers build and minimize claims. That work confirmed it was never where he belonged.

He founded the Romano Law Firm on one principle: represent only victims, never insurance companies or defendants. No conflicts of interest. Every client is on the side that matters — yours.

What does "no win, no fee" mean?
Our fee is contingent on your recovery. If we do not win your case, you owe us nothing in attorney's fees. You can pursue full legal representation without any upfront cost — we are compensated only if and when we recover for you.
Do I have to come to your office?
No. We offer office appointments in Florida, Washington D.C., and Oklahoma — but we also make home visits, hospital bedside visits, and webcam appointments. We accommodate your situation so you can focus on your recovery.
Do you ever represent insurance companies?
Never. We represent only individuals — only you. We never represent insurance companies or defendants. This means we have no conflicts of interest, and our full loyalty is always to our client.
My injury seems minor — should I still call?
Yes. Injuries that initially seem minor can prove to be more serious or financially significant over time. We evaluate each client on a case-by-case basis. A delay in seeking legal counsel can also jeopardize your claim due to filing deadlines. Our consultation is free, so there is no risk in calling early.
Do you work with other specialist attorneys?
Yes. The Romano Law Firm works with attorneys who specialize in related areas such as social security, workers' compensation, and product liability. When existing litigation or a class action applies to your situation, we can direct your claim to the right specialists.
